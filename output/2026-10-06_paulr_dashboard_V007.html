4. Backend Keyword Analysis

Every drop ASIN's live UK listing rows. Content Status comes from Amazon's own listing-issue feed. Backend keywords are the current values of the listing's search-term field.

Keyword Optimization Principle. The objective is not to replace the entire keyword strategy. The existing relevant keywords are retained, while duplicate, repeated and unnecessary words are removed. Nothing is added, re-ordered, translated or rewritten.

5. Keyword Change Details

One row per submitted backend keyword change (POST Accepted, under monitoring). Original Backend Keywords are kept for audit. Removed words are struck through in place, and the proposed cleaned field is shown under it. Every unique word from the original is kept.

Note: the keyword changes are limited to backend search terms. No visible listing content is changed by this process.

7. Change Record

A POST Accepted submission means the cleaned backend keywords were updated for the user: the build sets it to Monitoring straight away, with Date Changed = the monitoring start (update) date. A later Listing Management read is audit information only and never blocks monitoring. Records that were not submitted stay Proposed with no Date Changed. Completed can be saved once Week 2 is complete.

8. 7-Day Monitoring Report

Week 1 and Week 2 orders are calculated only after all 7 days of that week are loaded. Until then they stay “—” and the Result shows the monitoring week and how many of its 7 days are available. No performance verdict is given before Week 2 is complete.

12. Weekly Backend Keyword Check

Scope: the report's 24 ASIN-SKU rows (26 listings), never all seller listings. Each due listing (once per weekly cycle) gets a fresh GET of its current live backend keywords. A GET that shows the accepted keywords, or still the keywords from before an accepted POST, is recorded as audit only: the accepted payload is never re-sent and monitoring continues. Only a genuinely new value (for example the user's manual change) is cleaned of duplicate words, repeated entries and whitespace (every relevant word kept in order, nothing added) and POSTed if it differs; that POST Accepted starts a new monitoring cycle. Every GET, POST attempt and read-back is kept in the audit history. No e-mail is sent.
