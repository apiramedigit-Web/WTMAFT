4. Backend Keyword Analysis

Every drop ASIN's live UK listing rows. Content Status comes from Amazon's own listing-issue feed. Backend keywords are the current values of the listing's search-term field.

Keyword Optimization Principle. The objective is not to replace the entire keyword strategy. The existing relevant keywords are retained, while duplicate, repeated and unnecessary words are removed. Nothing is added, re-ordered, translated or rewritten.

5. Keyword Change Details

Original Backend Keywords are kept for audit. Removed words are struck through in place, and the proposed cleaned field is shown under it. Every unique word from the original is kept.

Note: the keyword changes are limited to backend search terms. No visible listing content is changed by this process.

7. Change Record

When a backend keyword change has actually been made in Amazon, enter the real Date Changed, set Status to Monitoring and click Save. The saved date starts the 7-day monitoring in Section 8. Proposed records are never monitored.

8. 7-Day Monitoring Report

Post-change figures are calculated only after all 7 post-change days are loaded. Until then they stay “—” and the Result shows how many of the 7 days are available.
